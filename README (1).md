# UK Labour Market Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing UK labour market trends using official ONS (Office for National Statistics) datasets.

The dashboard provides insights into employment, unemployment, workforce participation, regional labour trends, and sector-wise earnings across the United Kingdom.

---

## Business Problem

Understanding labour market trends is essential for governments, policymakers, businesses, and economists to support workforce planning and economic decision-making.

This dashboard helps analyze:
- Employment growth trends
- Unemployment patterns
- Regional workforce disparities
- Sector-based earnings
- Labour force composition

---

## Objectives

- Analyze employment and unemployment trends over time
- Compare workforce participation across UK regions
- Visualize labour force composition
- Identify regional employment disparities
- Provide KPI-driven labour market insights

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Excel
- ONS Open Data

---

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive filters and navigation
- Employment & unemployment trend analysis
- Regional workforce comparison
- Labour force composition analysis
- Sector-wise earnings visualization
- KPI monitoring dashboards
- UK regional map visualizations

---

## KPIs Analyzed

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Employment Rate | Percentage of employed workforce |
| Unemployment Rate | Percentage of unemployed population |
| Total Employment | Total employed individuals |
| Total Unemployment | Total unemployed individuals |
| AWE Total Pay | Average weekly earnings |

---

## Key Insights

- Employment levels showed long-term growth across the UK
- Unemployment rates declined significantly after economic recovery periods
- London and South East regions demonstrated stronger employment levels
- Sector-wise earnings varied significantly across industries
- Regional labour market disparities remained visible across the UK
